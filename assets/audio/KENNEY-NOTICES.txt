Kenney sound effects used by Burrow Brigade

Selected cues from Interface Sounds, Impact Sounds, RPG Audio and Sci-fi Sounds.
Creator: Kenney (https://kenney.nl)
License: Creative Commons Zero 1.0 Universal (CC0-1.0)
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Canonical converted PCM masters, source URLs and SHA-256 hashes are retained in
assets/audio/source/kenney/ in the source repository. Runtime copies are used
for interface, equipment, throw, impact, explosion, beam and launch cues.

Original pack notices follow.
