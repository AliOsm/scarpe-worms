### Weapon physics across the arsenal

Light taps now make short throws. Bouncing bombs continue moving after contact,
roll on slopes and use the last movement tick of their fuse. Fast, shallow
rockets and suitable thrown bombs can skip across water while losing energy;
steep or slow shots sink.

Blasts launch grubs into clearer arcs, followed by a landing rebound and slide.
The camera follows that motion, contact cues mark rebounds, and blast-triggered
mines and barrels allow a short delay before the next explosion. Water skips
also show a directional wake.

The arsenal now uses individual physical profiles for all 48 weapons and tools.
Launcher and bomb arcs, gun push, melee launch, air-support drops, fragments,
critters and loose props have distinct responses. Some profiles preserve their
existing values, including rope and jetpack motion; utility items retain their
individual rules. Damage values are unchanged.

Quick Battle includes the new host physics. Online matches use the host's
version, so update self-hosted servers as well as clients for the new tuning
and matching aim dots. Protocol 4 and existing saves remain supported; restored
shots retain their velocity and fuse, then continue under the new physics.

The tuning is inspired by the supplied gameplay references, with original
constants for Burrow Brigade. Automated gameplay checks cover short throws,
rebounds, water skims and saved-match recovery. Physical-device feel, audio and
balance still benefit from player testing; Mac timing benchmarks on shared CI
runners are informational.
