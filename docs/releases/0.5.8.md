Burrow Brigade 0.5.8 makes battlefield camera navigation easier to use.

- **Minimap dragging.** Click the minimap to jump to a location, then drag inside
  it to move the camera view area. This keeps the camera in manual mode and does
  not charge or fire the selected weapon.
- **Pinch zoom.** On macOS, a two-finger trackpad pinch now zooms the battlefield
  around the pointer. The existing mouse-wheel and Q/R controls remain available.
- **Cleaner camera controls.** The separate on-screen zoom buttons are removed;
  the map and follow controls remain in the battle HUD.

Existing settings, saved matches and protocol 4 remain compatible. The desktop
clients remain preview builds; macOS is ad-hoc signed and unnotarized, and the
Windows installer is unsigned.
