### Two-finger camera panning

The battle camera now responds to two-finger trackpad swipes. Swipe left, right,
up or down to pan the free camera in that direction; pinch still zooms around
the pointer, and ordinary mouse-wheel zoom is unchanged. Minimap and battlefield
dragging continue to provide direct camera movement, and **F / Follow** restores
the camera's automatic focus.

The native event path preserves precise horizontal and vertical trackpad axes
and keeps the existing three-argument wheel subscription shape for ordinary
wheel events. Protocol 4, existing saves and server/client compatibility remain
unchanged.
