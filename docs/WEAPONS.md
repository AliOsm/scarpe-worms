## Controls by stage

- Movement: A/D or ←/→ walks; W or Enter jumps; B backflips. While a rope or
  jetpack is active, W/S controls that tool. Arrow keys choose the approach for
  air strikes and the tilt for girders; A/D still walks with those selected.
- Thrown weapons: hold Space or left mouse, release to fire. Guns, mortar, tools,
  and critters fire on the press. Auto-repeat never counts as another press.
- Homing: click to lock a destination, move the cursor or use Up/Down to aim the
  launch, then charge with Space. Moving the cursor does not move the lock.
- Captain wool: tap to release, tap to launch upward, ←/→ or A/D turns in flight,
  tap again to explode. It flies at 260 pixels/s and turns at 2.7 radians/s.
  Takeoff starts an eight-second flight timer; after that it falls for at most
  four seconds, still detonatable. Collision explodes it; water ends the action.
- Tunnel buddy: tap to release, tap to jump and dive, tap to detonate while diving
  or digging. It also explodes after breaking out of the ground.
- Woolly menace: tap to release, tap to detonate. Stink express: tap to release,
  tap to spray poison along its path for four seconds, ending in gas and a small
  blast. Cattle call deploys three runners 0.8 seconds apart; walls or grubs trigger
  each blast, with an eight-second fallback timer per cow.
- Grapple: aim with the mouse or Up/Down, then press Space or click. A visible hook
  travels up to 460 pixels and catches the first terrain or girder in its path;
  aim away from straight down. A miss retracts for free. Once hooked, A/D or
  Left/Right pumps the swing; W/S or Up/Down reels between 28 and 460 pixels.
  Space, Enter, or B releases without changing velocity. Fire again while airborne
  to re-hook using the same cartridge, including the last one; landing ends that
  use. Destroyed anchors detach. The rope has one anchor and no corner wrapping.
- Jetpack: W/Up supplies lift; A/D or Left/Right thrusts sideways. Five seconds of
  fuel burns only during upward or horizontal thrust. S/Down and released keys
  coast under gravity. Speed is capped at 220 pixels/s upward and 210 sideways.
  Space stops the pack and arms a parachute; fuel exhaustion does the same.
- Parachute: Space or click equips it for this turn. It opens automatically on
  descent, slows falls to 65 pixels/s, and prevents fall damage while open. A/D or
  Left/Right steers with wind drift. The visible canopy closes on landing and
  reopens on another fall that turn.
- Teleport: the landing ghost shows where the grub will stand. The preview and
  host share checks for body clearance, other grubs, and water; invalid targets
  show red and cost no ammo. Successful teleportation ends the turn.
- Girder: Left/Right rotates the preview in 22.5° steps up to vertical. Red means
  the shared host/preview checks reject the beam's terrain, grub, or water overlap.
  A valid placement ends the turn and becomes collision terrain. Beam length is
  fixed at 140 pixels; there is no short/long toggle.
- Digging: tap Space or click to start. Space or another click stops early;
  Up/Down changes the blowtorch slope. Face the tunnel before starting Side hustle.
  The HUD shows the tool's remaining time. Digging uses the turn.
- Double tap: each press fires one shot. Re-aim for the second; it belongs to the
  same cartridge, including the last cartridge. Finish both before switching.
- Guns: aim with the mouse or Up/Down, then tap Space or click. Hot breath needs
  a nearby target; its stream leaves fire that keeps burning after the spray ends.
- Close-up: move within reach, aim toward the grub, then tap Space or click.
  Home run launches along the aim angle; Dragon dash carves through the ground ahead.
- Hazards: face the placement, then tap Space or click to place just ahead.
  Retreat with A/D; an armed mine reacts to either team's grubs.
- Bubble wrap, Extra spicy, Moon boots and Weather vane keep the turn. The HUD
  says so; open the arsenal with E to follow a utility with an attack. First aid,
  Ice break and Pass the turn use the turn.

The HUD shows the current stage, next action, fuse, rope length, or remaining
thrust. Irrelevant controls are disabled. Focus loss, menus, weapon changes, and
turn expiry cancel charging. A mouse release cannot release a keyboard charge.
A pending action waits for the host's next authoritative snapshot before a second
activation can be sent. The server validates ownership, parameters, phase, and ammo.

## Munitions details

Air support spends one charge for an entire pass. The bomber releases five objects
at 0.4-second intervals in the chosen approach direction, covering about 152 world
pixels around the clicked point. Zooming or resizing preserves the ongoing pass.
Mines take 40 ticks (1.33 seconds) to arm after
landing; nearby grubs then trigger an 18-tick warning. Napalm waits for its loose
flames to burn out before handing over the turn. Bad astronomy scatters six
meteors across the map in 0.3-second intervals. All four sky attacks are blocked
in caverns.

Choir bomb keeps moving after its fixed five-second count until it rests, then
plays a 0.6-second warning before exploding. Its complete lifetime is bounded by
12 seconds. Short fuse always counts four seconds, regardless of the last chosen
throwing fuse. The HUD distinguishes those fixed fuses from adjustable ones.

Mortar and Party popper create five small impact fragments; Fruit salad creates
six. Fragments retain part of the parent's horizontal momentum and carry damage
boosts, but never split again. Their small sprites have no fuse badge. All thrown
weapons have dedicated in-flight art, slow tumbling, readable countdowns and
weapon-specific blasts. Rocket, homing and meteor trails follow their flight;
Choir bomb glows and dynamite sparks. Camera shake is bounded and disabled by
the reduced-motion preference.

Velcro bomb follows an attached grub through the final fuse tick, so a moving
target carries the blast with it. It drops free when its target dies or its
terrain support disappears. Fuse sparks and canister wisps rotate with their
source rather than floating beside a tumbling bomb.

## Match options

Rules are locked during a match. Changing a lobby rule clears all human ready checks.
Import/export preserves the seed and every ammo override. All values are validated.

| Option | Default | Supported values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| seed | little-havoc | 1–40 printable characters |
| biome | meadow | meadow, desert, glacier, volcano |
| terrain | islands | islands, archipelago, cavern |
| scheme | full | full, classic, sandbox |

| water rise | 12 | 0..40 |

Maps use hills and underground hollows (islands), separated land masses (archipelago),
or an enclosed ceiling (cavern). Every biome is cosmetic: collision uses the same
material mask. Caverns disable air support. Craters and bridges alter that mask.

After the configured round limit, surviving grubs drop to one health and water
rises each turn. Setting the water rise to zero permits longer standoffs. A grub
entering water is eliminated regardless of health. Poison does not reduce health
below one; protection effects expire on the team's next turn.

Up/Down adjusts aim, reels the rope, or controls the active tool; W/Up supplies
lift with an active jetpack. W jumps when neither rope nor jetpack is active.
The arsenal describes
targeted tools and whether they use a turn. Hover a grub in a crowded battle
to reveal its name; the active grub always shows its name.
